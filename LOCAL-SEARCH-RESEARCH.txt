4. QUESTIONS THAT HELP CUSTOMERS AND ANSWER ENGINES

Write plain, directly answered questions using only confirmed facts:
- Where is Cyclonite Tech based?
- What underwater services can the team provide?
- Can you assess a project in Lumut or elsewhere in Perak?
- What information should I send to request a quotation?
- Can you assess diving equipment or marine electronics?
- How are underwater projects planned before mobilisation?
- Where can I view examples of your work?

Each answer should be visible in HTML, meaningful without images and linked to the relevant service/contact page. Do not promise fixed mobilisation times, prices, approvals or 24-hour availability without business confirmation. FAQs are a customer usability/content choice, not a guaranteed search-result feature.

5. TECHNICAL SEO AND GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE LAUNCH PLAN

A. Publish the chosen production domain over HTTPS. Ensure important pages are publicly accessible, return HTTP 200, contain useful indexable text and permit Googlebot. Remove preview noindex settings only from production pages intended for indexing. Keep true missing pages as 404s. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing.
Google: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical

B. Set one absolute canonical URL per indexable page using the final production domain. Align internal links, redirects and sitemap URLs; do not canonicalise all service pages to the home page. Canonicals are signals, not guarantees of Google's selection. Redirect duplicate www/non-www and protocol variants appropriately.
Google: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls

C. Generate a UTF-8 XML sitemap containing canonical production URLs, serve it from the root and reference it in robots.txt. Include only real URLs intended for search. Submit it through Search Console after verification. Sitemaps help discovery; they do not guarantee indexing or rankings.
Google: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap

D. Verify a Domain property using DNS when domain administration is available. A URL-prefix property can use Google's supplied HTML verification tag or file. Never invent a verification token; use the exact account-generated value and keep it installed. The live verification resource must be accessible without login. This website can be prepared for verification, but code alone cannot establish account ownership.
Google: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080?hl=en

E. In Search Console, inspect the home, service, portfolio and contact URLs; check selected canonicals, crawl access and page indexing. Request indexing for key pages after launch and monitor sitemap processing. Use Performance data to prioritise real queries and pages, with country/device filters. Search Console is not a map-grid rank tracker and does not prove a specific Manjung street-level position.

Implementation recommendations: descriptive unique titles and meta descriptions; semantic headings; descriptive crawlable navigation; authentic image alt text; responsive images; image dimensions to limit layout shift; lazy-load below-fold images; avoid heavy autoplay hero videos; keep essential content independent of animation. Use accurate LocalBusiness/Organization, Service and BreadcrumbList data only where the displayed content supports it. Do not add invented ratings, geographic coordinates, opening hours or certifications.

6. LOCAL VISIBILITY ACTION PLAN

Google identifies relevance, distance and prominence/popularity as the main local ranking factors. Complete, accurate business information, verification, photos and genuine reviews can help. There is no way to pay Google for a better local organic ranking. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en

Recommended next actions (not performed by this research):
1. Confirm the exact business name, phone, address, hours, service coverage and whether customers can visit the office.
2. Claim/verify the eligible Google Business Profile. Choose the most accurate available primary category; add only applicable secondary categories and services. Keep the real name free of added search keywords.
3. Align website contact details and other legitimate company listings. Use the real map pin; use appropriate service-area settings if the business visits customers and does not receive them at the address.
4. Add real team, equipment, premises and project photos with permission. Publish genuine local case studies with actual outcomes.
5. Invite completed-project customers to leave honest reviews, without incentives or screening out criticism; reply helpfully.
6. Seek legitimate industry associations, supplier/partner listings and editorial coverage where there is a real relationship. Do not buy spam links or manufacture mentions.
7. Track qualified WhatsApp conversations, call clicks and enquiries separately from rankings. An outbound click is not proof of an enquiry or a sale.

7. CURRENT AEO / GENERATIVE SEARCH GUIDANCE

Google's current guide says normal SEO foundations remain relevant; there is no special schema needed for generative AI search. It explicitly says llms.txt is ignored by Google Search and neither helps nor harms its rankings. Original, useful service/project information and accurate business details are better investments than rewriting for every keyword variant. These recommendations improve the site's usefulness but do not guarantee AI citations.
Google: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

Important 2026 change: Google stopped showing FAQ rich results from 7 May 2026 and removed that documentation on 15 June 2026. Do not sell FAQPage markup as a Google rich-result benefit. Retain helpful visible FAQs as ordinary page content.
Google changelog: https://developers.google.com/search/updates

Google's Generative AI performance report now covers AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions. Official documentation says it rolled out worldwide by 31 August 2026; a site may not see the report until it has enough impressions. Use it to monitor page, country and device visibility, not as proof of booked projects or a guaranteed AI ranking.
Google: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139
